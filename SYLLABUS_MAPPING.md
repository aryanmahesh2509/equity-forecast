## Main source-specific observations

- The Unit 1 slide deck covers pattern exploration, seasonal/cyclic distinctions, lag plots, ACF, white noise, decomposition, moving-average smoothing and stationarity.
- Unit 2 covers regression forecasting, least squares and residual checks, AIC/AICc/BIC, SES, Holt trend and Holt–Winters additive/multiplicative methods.
- The combined Units 3–6 deck explicitly covers random walks, differencing, AR/MA/ARIMA, ACF/PACF, ADF, Auto ARIMA, seasonal ARIMA, prediction intervals, test-set evaluation, Ljung–Box, regression with ARIMA errors, SMA and WMA.
- Unit 7 covers hierarchical/grouped time series, top-down/bottom-up/middle-out approaches and reconciliation, including OLS and MinT. Those topics are relevant to the course but not to a single unaggregated ticker series.
- The original notebook uses TCS.NS, `auto_adjust=True`, business-day reindexing with forward filling, percentage returns, a fixed 85/15 split and manually specified ARIMA(2,0,2). Its walk-forward section refits using observed returns, but its forecast-price indexing and compounding need care; see `BUG_AUDIT.md`.
